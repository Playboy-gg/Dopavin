## What's new in Dopavin 1.1

**Player and animations**
- Predictive back for Now Playing: the player follows your finger, holds at the top of the screen and slides away when you let go.
- The album cover now flies between the mini player and Now Playing, and settles into the mini player with a soft spring.
- Swipe down anywhere on the artwork to close the player; the bottom bar and mini player stay put instead of being rebuilt.
- Pages slide back instead of cross-fading, so the previous screen no longer flashes. Settings categories now use the same back gesture.
- Swipe the mini player sideways to skip songs; it stretches towards your finger.

**New features**
- **Recap**: minutes listened, plays, top songs and artists for 7 days, 30 days or all time (Library > Recap).
- **Share song** and **Share artist**.
- **Lyrics card**: pick up to six lines, choose a style and share them as an image.
- **Show current lyric** under the artist name in the full player (Settings > Streaming & Lyrics, off by default).
- **Home-screen widget** with artwork, title and previous / play-pause / next.
- **Import from Spotify**: paste a public playlist or album link and Dopavin finds the songs on YouTube Music (Library > Import from Spotify).
- **Download all** and **Add all to queue** for radio mixes and playlists, behind a new ⋯ menu next to Play and Shuffle.
- **Play on…** in Now Playing > More opens the system output switcher.
- **Updates**: Settings > Updates tells you when a new version is on GitHub, shows what is new, and downloads and installs it inside the app (Android asks you to confirm).

**Settings**
- Separate streaming quality for mobile data.
- Don't repeat suggestions (autoplay skips what you heard recently).
- Haptic feedback switch (Appearance).
- Clear lyrics cache (Settings > Storage).

**Search and library**
- "Show more results" paging in search.
- Better lyrics matching, karaoke-style word highlighting, and a crash report screen if something goes wrong.

Dopavin is under active development; please report problems on the issue tracker.
