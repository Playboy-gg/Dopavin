## What's new in Dopavin 1.2

**Home that fits you**
- Music from where you are: the charts are your own country's, no longer a default. Dopavin reads your country and your phone's languages by itself; it never asks you to choose a region or a language.
- Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Urdu, Spanish, Arabic, Korean and more: if your phone speaks it, Home offers it. Playing songs in another language moves that language to the front.
- An English phone in India keeps the Hindi and Punjabi tiles; a phone in the United States gets English.

**New here**
- A short welcome on the first launch shows what Dopavin does and lets you tap a few artists (or skip) so Home is yours from the first minute. Your picks stay on your phone.

**Updates**
- When a new version is available, Dopavin now opens an Update available dialog: what is new, how big it is, and one button to download and install. Choose Later, or skip that version.

**Player and queue**
- The Now Playing cover is shown whole, with a soft rounded shadow.
- Drag a song in the queue: it stays inside the list and the list scrolls by itself at the top and bottom edge.
- Autoplay keeps about ten songs ready. Every queue row shows its cover and length, also after a restart.
- Streamed autoplay, radio and mood queues no longer mix in your downloaded songs unless you are offline, and the same song is not queued twice.

**Lyrics**
- Word-synced lyrics come first, then line-synced, then plain text. Dopavin keeps looking for a better kind before settling for a lower one.
- Better search: a title like "Song (Official Video)" is now also searched as plain "Song", songs by labels and channels are found by their singer, and Hindi and Punjabi titles match properly.
- If lyrics cannot be loaded you now see a plain message, not a technical error.

**Smoother and steadier**
- Fewer needless redraws, so scrolling and the player stay fluid, also while songs download. Covers load faster and are remembered between launches.
- Fixed a crash on Android 12 and newer when playback started in the background, for example when headphones reconnect.

**About the project**
- Settings > Contributors shows the people behind Dopavin with their GitHub pictures: playboy-gg (creator and maintainer) and Sandeepbedia (UI/UX designer). Tap a name to visit their profile.

Dopavin is under active development; please report problems on the issue tracker.
